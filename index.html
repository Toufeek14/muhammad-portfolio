04 / Sample Strategy

30-day digital marketing plan

Objective: help a hypothetical small business improve awareness and generate relevant enquiries through consistent, useful social media content.

Week 1 — Understand

Define the target audience, review competitors, identify customer needs, establish a baseline, and refine the brand message.

Week 2 — Publish

Share introductory posts, product or service explainers, short videos, and educational content with consistent branding.

Week 3 — Engage

Answer questions, invite audience feedback, address common concerns, and test different calls to action.

Week 4 — Evaluate

Review content performance, compare results with the starting baseline, identify strong topics, and recommend improvements.

Target audience

Potential customers in the business's service area who are likely to need its products or services. Final targeting depends on research.

Primary channels

Instagram and Facebook for discovery and engagement, with WhatsApp or a suitable landing page for customer enquiries where appropriate.
